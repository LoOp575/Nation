# Anti-Mafia Intelligence

## 1. Purpose

Monitor the use of public funds and the integrity of public projects, procurement, contracts, ownership disclosures, delivery, and public benefit. The module is an evidence-led public-accountability monitor, not an automated accusation or law-enforcement system.

## 2. Monitoring lifecycle

1. Project proposal and planning
2. Budget allocation and approval
3. Policy/official decision timeline
4. Procurement plan and tender
5. Bidder and supplier records
6. Award and contract
7. Amendments, variations, and subcontracting
8. Payments and budget realization
9. Physical delivery and quality
10. Audit findings and follow-up
11. Public outcome and benefit assessment

Preserve the timeline and source record for each stage.

## 4. Entity model

Entities may include:
- public project
- government agency
- office holder
- supplier/company
- beneficial owner
- bidder
- subcontractor
- contract
- budget line
- tender
- audit record
- evidence document

Each entity needs a stable internal ID, official identifiers where lawfully available, aliases, validity dates, and source-backed attributes. Entity matching must account for similar names and changing office titles. Uncertain matches remain unresolved; do not force a link.

## 5. Risk signals to examine

- unusually low number of bidders
- repeated awards concentrated among suppliers
- material contract amendments
- unexplained cost escalation
- restrictive or unusually specific tender requirements
- related-party or ownership links
- mismatch between payment and delivery
- repeated project delays or quality failures
- audit findings without visible follow-up
- project outcomes materially below stated objectives

These are screening signals, not proof of corruption. Each signal requires context, baseline comparison, source references, and counter-evidence.

## 6. Decision and personnel timeline

A timeline may record a public official's statement, objection, appointment, dismissal, policy decision, or approval only when supported by a source. Record:
- person's verified identity
- office and validity dates
- exact action or attributed statement
- date of event and date of publication
- source and evidence excerpt
- whether the statement is direct, paraphrased, or reported by another party

A minister's disagreement followed by dismissal and continuation of a project is a sequence worth documenting, but does not itself establish improper influence or criminal conduct.

## 7. Beneficial ownership and relationship safeguards

Use only lawfully accessible and sufficiently reliable ownership information. Record source, effective date, ownership type, percentage if published, and verification status. Distinguish direct ownership, beneficial ownership, management, family relationship, business association, and mere name similarity. Do not infer undisclosed relationships.

## 8. Finding statuses

- RECORDED: a sourced event or document exists
- NEEDS_REVIEW: a signal requires further checking
- ELEVATED_RISK_SIGNAL: multiple relevant indicators meet a documented rule
- UNDER_OFFICIAL_REVIEW: an identified authority has publicly confirmed a review/investigation
- OFFICIAL_LEGAL_FINDING: a specific official legal outcome exists; state the exact status and authority

Never use a generic "mafia" or "corrupt" label for a person/company as an automated status.

## 9. Evidence and counter-evidence

Every case should contain:
- claim/question being examined
- supporting evidence
- counter-evidence and alternative explanations
- source reliability and independence
- timeline
- methodology/rule version
- confidence and unresolved gaps
- analyst notes and review status
- correction/right-of-reply record

AI-generated leads must be marked as leads until reviewed.

## 10. Monitoring horizon

Track cases over 1–5 months and longer where useful. New evidence should append to the timeline; it must not rewrite the historical record. Alerts should explain what changed since the previous review.

## 11. Publication and privacy

Separate public records from internal analyst notes and restricted personal data. Minimize personal data, restrict access, log access, and obtain legal review before publishing sensitive personal allegations. Provide a correction and response channel.
